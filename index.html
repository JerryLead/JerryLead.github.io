Brief Biography

         I am currently a professor at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences.
        I was a senior researcher in the Systems group, ETH Zürich, working with Prof. Gustavo Alonso and previously with Prof. Ce Zhang.
        My research interests focus on AI+Data Frameworks, including DB4AI, AI+BigData, LLM Agent frameworks, etc. Currently, I'm working on LLM Agent frameworks as well as AI+DB/BigData frameworks.
